# Phase 1 demo checklist (click through on your phone)

1. **Sign in.** Open the app and enter kevin@honesttransportation.com. Use the email link or type the 6-digit code. You land on Home as **Admin**.
2. **Home.** Check the KPI tiles, the pipeline and strategy bars, your tasks, and recent activity. The auction and stale-lead alerts appear once imported data has dates.
3. **Find a parcel.** In the top search, type `836-121` and open **836-121-003 (1340 W Hobson Way)**.
4. **Change status.** Set Status to *Owner found*. The Activity tab shows `researching → owner_found`, and History shows the field change with you as the author.
5. **@mention.** Tap **Add note** and type `@` to pick a teammate, then save. The note appears in Activity, and the teammate's bell shows "…mentioned you on 836-121-003".
6. **Task.** Tap **Add task**, give it a due date, and save. It shows on the Tasks tab, on /tasks, and on the teammate's Home if you assigned it to them.
7. **PDF.** Tap **Upload doc**, pick a PDF, and save. It appears on the Documents tab (tap to preview) and as "Uploaded …" in Activity.
8. **Gates.** Try moving the parcel to *Offer made*. You're asked to record the offer first. *Under contract* asks for the contract PDF, and *Dead* asks for a reason.
9. **Pipeline.** Drag a card to another column. The status changes and the move is logged.
10. **Import.** Go to Imports and upload `2025 PTS INVENTORY - TAG ORDER.xlsx`.
    - The format and header row 4 are detected. Preview shows new / matched / conflicts / errors.
    - Commit it.
    - Re-upload the same file: it creates 0 new parcels, so no duplicates.
11. **Table.** On Properties, open Filters → I-10 Corridor + Acres ≥ 5.
    - Save the view as shared.
    - Change the columns and drag them to reorder.
    - Bulk-assign a few rows.
    - Export CSV.
12. **Settings.** Once teammates have signed in, set their roles.
